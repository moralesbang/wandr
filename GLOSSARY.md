# Wandr

Wandr is a personal walking journal and a collective atlas of small discoveries. These terms distinguish a person's experience from the discoveries and reusable routes it can inspire.

## Language

**Walk**:
A personal experience of going for a walk, preserved as a journal entry. It can exist without GPS geometry, photographs, or Finds.
_Avoid_: Route when referring to an individual walking experience; workout as the default name.

**Find**:
Something discovered that is worth preserving through a photograph or text. It can exist independently of a Walk and without a location.
_Avoid_: Post as a synonym for the discovery itself.

**Route**:
A reusable walking proposal, distinct from the personal experience of a Walk. It describes a path someone can explore on a future outing.
_Avoid_: Walk when referring to a reusable path; private track as a synonym for the public Route.

**Save**:
A person's private reference to a Find or Route they want to revisit. It is not a copy of the content and does not preserve access after the content is withdrawn.
_Avoid_: Collection when referring to the unorganized saved list.

**Public Walk journal**:
The selected, explicitly published account of a Walk. It is separate from the private Walk and does not include its recorded path or private activity measurements.
_Avoid_: Public track when referring to the shared journal.

**Streak**:
A chain of Walks connected by Wandr's habit-continuation window. Its value is a count of Walks, not a count of consecutive calendar days.
_Avoid_: Daily streak; consecutive days when describing this metric.

**Recorded steps**:
Steps measured during Walks recorded with Wandr. They describe the activity captured by the app, not a person's total daily movement.
_Avoid_: Total daily steps when referring only to Wandr recordings; estimated steps as a synonym for measured steps.
